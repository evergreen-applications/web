Privacy Policy

What we collect, and what we don't.

1. Overview

LaunchPlus, built by Evergreen Applications, is designed with minimal data collection in mind. The App does not require you to create an account or log in to use it.

2. Information We Collect

Launch notification signups: if you sign up on this website to be notified when LaunchPlus launches, we collect the email address you provide via our form provider, Jotform.

In-app usage: [describe any analytics, crash reporting, or device data collected by the App here once finalized — e.g., anonymous, aggregated analytics via <provider>. If LaunchPlus collects no in-app data, state that explicitly.]

3. How We Use Information

We use the email address you provide only to send you a one-time notification when LaunchPlus becomes available, and any closely related launch updates. We do not sell your information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The launch notification form is hosted by Jotform; their handling of submitted data is governed by Jotform's Privacy Policy. The App itself displays data from NASA's public APIs (including the Astronomy Picture of the Day) and links to YouTube for livestreams; use of those services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

We retain launch notification email addresses only until LaunchPlus launches and the announcement has been sent, or until you ask us to remove your information sooner.

6. Your Choices

To have your email address removed from our launch notification list at any time, contact us via the Support page.

7. Children's Privacy

LaunchPlus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach out via the Support page.
