Frequently Asked Questions

When Does SpaceHub Launch?

SpaceHub is currently in development. Sign up on the home page to be notified the moment it's available.

Do I Need An Account To Use SpaceHub?

No. SpaceHub works right away with no login and no account required.

What Platforms Will SpaceHub Support?

SpaceHub is being built for iPhone.

Where Does The Launch And Tracking Data Come From?

SpaceHub pulls from public spaceflight data sources and NASA's public APIs, including the Astronomy Picture of the Day, to keep launch schedules, ISS position, and other information up to date.

I Found A Bug Or Have A Feature Request. What Do I Do?

Email us at support@evergreenapplications.app with as much detail as you can — screenshots help a lot.
